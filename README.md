## Who it's for

- **Students and career-switchers**: understand AI news without a CS degree. Headlines are in plain language, and every story has an **Explain it simply** button.
- **Developers and builders**: know which launches are worth your time and skip the hype.
- **Founders and managers**: catch up in **60 seconds of audio** on your commute, with a "why it matters" line for every story.
- **Anyone who feels behind**: a short list you can finish, then close the app.

## What you get

- **Today in AI**: a 60-second audio bulletin of the day's biggest stories, like a news broadcast.
- **Ranked, not endless**: only the top stories of the day, sorted by importance. No infinite scroll.
- **Why it matters**: every story says what happened and why you should care.
- **Explain it simply**: a beginner-friendly version of any story, with key terms defined.
- **Your topics**: choose from models, companies, tools, research, business, robotics, hardware and regulation, and your picks come first.
- **Alerts that earn their buzz**: a morning briefing notification and alerts for major news only. Turn either off in Settings.

## Where the news comes from

AI lab blogs and trusted tech press, checked every hour. Each story links back to its source so you can read the original.

## Install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/Mlakshay01/ai-news-app/releases/latest) on your Android phone and download the `.apk`.
2. Open it and allow "Install unknown apps" if Android asks. This is normal for apps outside the Play Store.
3. Open **AI News** and allow notifications.

## Beta notes

- To update, uninstall the old version first. Saved stories will be lost.
- Summaries are AI-written. Check the source link for anything important.
